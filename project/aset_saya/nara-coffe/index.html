Our Story

It started with
a cup.

NARA began with a simple belief — that coffee deserves to be experienced, not rushed.

Founded in 2026 in Jakarta, NARA was born from a desire to create a space where time slows down. A place where the ritual of coffee-making becomes an act of presence.

We source our beans directly from farmers who share our commitment to quality. Every roast is done in small batches, every brew is measured with care.

"We wanted to create a place that feels like a deep breath. Where the aroma of freshly brewed coffee reminds you to be here, now."

— The Founders of NARA
