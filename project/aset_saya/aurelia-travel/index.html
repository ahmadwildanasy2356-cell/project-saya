Featured Itinerary

5 Days in Kyoto

Arrive at Kyoto Station, check into your accommodation near Gion. Evening walk through the historic geisha district, visiting Yasaka Shrine and strolling along Hanamikoji Street.

Visit Kinkaku-ji (Golden Pavilion) and Ryoan-ji Temple. Afternoon tea ceremony experience. Evening exploration of Nishiki Market for local street food.

Full day in Arashiyama — bamboo grove at sunrise, Tenryu-ji Temple, and a scenic walk along the Katsura River. Optional monkey park visit.

Explore hidden gems — Fushimi Inari Shrine at dawn, Tofuku-ji Temple gardens, and the quieter streets of Higashiyama. Evening at a local izakaya.

Leisurely breakfast, final souvenir shopping, and a quiet walk along the Kamogawa River before heading to Kyoto Station for departure.
