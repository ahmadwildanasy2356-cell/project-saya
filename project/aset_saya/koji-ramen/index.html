OUR STORY

IT STARTED
WITH A BOWL.

KŌJI RAMEN began with a simple belief: that a bowl of ramen can be more than food. It can be a moment of pause in a busy world. A ritual. A return to craft.

Our founders spent years studying the art of ramen — from the back alleys of Tokyo to the bustling ramen counters of Sapporo. They learned that great ramen isn't about shortcuts. It's about time, patience, and the courage to keep things simple.

Today, KŌJI RAMEN continues that tradition. Every broth is simmered slowly. Every noodle is made fresh. Every bowl is assembled with intention. Because we believe that in a world that never stops, a bowl of ramen should be worth slowing down for.

THE PROCESS

CRAFTED FROM
THE BOTTOM UP.

01

BROTH

Slow-simmered for 12+ hours. We use a blend of pork bones, chicken, and aromatics to create a rich, layered foundation.

02

NOODLES

Made fresh every morning. Our noodles are springy, chewy, and perfectly paired with each broth style.

03

TARE

The soul of the bowl. Our house tare is a careful blend of soy, miso, and secret spices that defines each ramen.

04

TOPPINGS

From tender chashu to jammy ajitama, each topping is prepared with the same care as the broth itself.

05

FINISH

The final assembly. Every bowl is composed with precision, served hot and ready to be savored.
